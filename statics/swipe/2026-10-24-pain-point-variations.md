## The post

> Stop thinking one pain point = done.
>
> The real game is finding the TOP pain point and building 10+ variations around it.
>
> Example:
>
> Pain point from research = "I can't stop checking my hairline"
>
> Turn it into:\
> → "If you've ever taken a photo of your hairline in direct sunlight just to check, this is for you"\
> → "Why I wore a hoodie to every party for two years"\
> → "The bathroom light at your gym isn't lying to you"\
> → "How I stopped combing my hair forward to hide my forehead"\
> → "Stop refreshing hair loss forums at 2am"\
> → "The real reason you buzzed it down to a #2 'because it's summer'"\
> → "Why you scared of finasteride but more scared of losing it"\
> → "Swimming pools, rain, and wet hair. The three things I avoided at 24."\
> → "Stop zooming into every group photo to check your crown"\
> → "I made fun of a buddy's receding hairline in college. Now I'm the one getting roasted."
>
> Same underlying issue in 10 different angles.

## The angle behind each line

None of the 10 lines repeat the research quote. Each one turns it into a specific moment, habit or feeling the reader recognises.

| # | Line | Angle |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Photo of your hairline in direct sunlight | **Secret checking ritual.** "If you've ever..., this is for you" call-out |
| 2 | Wore a hoodie to every party for two years | **Hiding behaviour**, told as a first-person story |
| 3 | The gym bathroom light isn't lying to you | **The moment of truth.** The place where denial stops working |
| 4 | Stopped combing my hair forward | **Cover-up habit**, with a "how I stopped" promise |
| 5 | Refreshing hair loss forums at 2am | **Late-night anxiety loop** |
| 6 | Buzzed it to a #2 "because it's summer" | **The excuse.** Calls out the cover story |
| 7 | Scared of finasteride, more scared of losing it | **Fear vs fear.** The objection to the obvious fix |
| 8 | Pools, rain, wet hair. Avoided at 24 | **Things I gave up.** A list with an age attached |
| 9 | Zooming into every group photo | **Checking ritual** in a social setting |
| 10 | Mocked a buddy's hairline, now it's me | **Irony / karma** story |

## How to run it

1. Pull the single most repeated pain point from research (reviews, comments, DMs, Reddit, sales chats). Keep the customer's exact words.
2. List the moments where that pain shows up. Use these prompts:
   - What do they **check** in secret?
   - What do they **hide** or cover up?
   - What do they **avoid** or give up?
   - What **excuse** do they tell other people?
   - When does it hit hardest (**time of day, place, lighting, event**)?
   - What are they **afraid** of in the obvious fix?
   - What's the **ironic or embarrassing** backstory?
3. Write one line per moment. Mix formats: "If you've ever...", "Why I...", "How I stopped...", "Stop...", "The real reason...".
4. Use each line as the hook or overlay on its own static. Keep the visual and offer the same so you're testing the angle.
