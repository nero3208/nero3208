## The post

> How a static ad hits $120k+ in spend:
>
> → Call out your specific audience [people on Mounjaro]\
> → Open a curiosity loop [this paragraph is an eye-opener]\
> → Validate the result they want [weight loss]\
> → Introduce the overlooked problem [loose skin after weight loss]\
> → Challenge the obvious solution [another skin cream]\
> → Reveal a new mechanism [Type I & III collagen]\
> → Make the solution stupidly simple [2 tablespoons in coffee]
>
> That's how you make a random book image print $2k/day lol.

**The boxed paragraph** (presented as "Dermatologists say:"):

> "The weight loss is only half the story. When it comes off that fast, your skin loses collagen quicker than the body can rebuild it. That's the loose skin, the hollow face. The fix isn't another cream. It's **giving your skin back the Type I & 3 collagen it's built from.** Just add two tablespoons to your coffee."

The rest of the page is soft, editorial filler about collagen and celebrating weight loss, which makes it read like a real book.

## How each step maps onto the ad

| Step | In the ad |
|---|---|
| 1. Call out the audience | "If you are on Mounjaro" in the overlay. Narrow, and self-selecting |
| 2. Open a curiosity loop | "this paragraph is an eye opener 👀". You have to read the box to close it |
| 3. Validate the result they want | "The weight loss is only half the story". It doesn't argue with the goal |
| 4. Introduce the overlooked problem | "loose skin, the hollow face". A side effect they're already worried about |
| 5. Challenge the obvious solution | "The fix isn't another cream" |
| 6. Reveal a new mechanism | Skin loses collagen faster than the body rebuilds it, so you put Type I & III back |
| 7. Make the solution stupidly simple | "Just add two tablespoons to your coffee" |

## Why the format works

- **It doesn't look like an ad.** A book page reads as content someone found, so it gets past ad blindness.
- **The red box and highlight tell you exactly where to look.** All seven steps sit inside about 60 words.
- **The overlay does steps 1 and 2 alone.** Someone who only reads the overlay still knows if it's for them.
- **The product is never shown.** The page sells the mechanism, and the landing page sells the product.

## Fill-in template

```
Overlay:   If you are [specific audience / situation], this paragraph is an eye opener 👀

Boxed paragraph:
"[Result they want] is only half the story. When [it happens / what they're doing],
[overlooked problem, said the way they'd say it]. The fix isn't [obvious solution
they've already tried]. It's [new mechanism, one line]. Just [tiny action] [where/when]."

Filler:    2-3 calm paragraphs that sound like a real book on the topic.
Visual:    Phone photo of an open book, warm light, red box + yellow highlight on the key line.
```

## Watch-outs before using it

- The ad attributes the quote to unnamed "Dermatologists". Only quote a real, attributable source, and keep claims to what you can back up.
- Naming a medication (Mounjaro) and implying a body or health outcome can trip ad platform health and personal-attribute policies. Test the call-out wording.
